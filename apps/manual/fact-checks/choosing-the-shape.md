# Fact-check: Choosing the shape: buy, adopt or build, how to plan, and when subagents pay

The claims log for [`choosing-the-shape.md`](../src/content/chapters/choosing-the-shape.md), checked section by section as each was drafted (BK21). Each row names the claim, the section it sits in, the verdict and the primary source. Pages were read on 2026-11-22. Package claims are checked against the harness pins of that date: `@openai/agents@0.21.0` for the handoff and subagent examples and `@google/adk@2.5.0` for the workflow examples. The two papers behind the planning section, ReAct and Reflexion, and the plan-then-execute guide were read in full as PDFs, not only as abstracts.

## The research question, answered before drafting

**Question.** When should a team buy a vendor harness, adopt a library SDK or build its own loop, which planning pattern should it start with, and when do subagents pay for themselves?

**Answer, from the todo's sources only.** Buy when the vendor's loop is the product you want, as for a coding or filesystem agent where a small team needs a fast start, and accept that you configure the loop and never replace it. Adopt a library loop for a chat or workflow agent or when you must stay portable across model vendors, because it runs in your process. Build when custom compaction, routing, cost control or portability is itself the product, and buy sandboxes and observability. The harness literature gives no reason to buy a loop for a better score, only for its cost profile and fit ([main report](../../../docs/research/agent-sdks-and-harness-design.md#build-vs-buy-depends-on-three-questions)). Start planning as a ReAct loop, the model reasoning and acting step by step, because it is the default of the SDKs we verified and no controlled benchmark ranks the planning patterns under identical models; add an upfront plan or a reflection pass only when a specific failure justifies the extra model calls ([main report](../../../docs/research/agent-sdks-and-harness-design.md#planning-should-start-as-react-and-earn-every-extra-agent), [agent research papers](../../../docs/research/papers/agent-research-papers.md)). Choose a workflow, a router or a handoff by who decides the next step: code, a classifier or the model ([Anthropic, Building effective agents](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/building-effective-agents)). Subagents pay when the work parallelises, exceeds one context window or needs many tools, and they cost about 15 times the tokens of a chat against about 4 times for one agent ([Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/multi-agent-research-system)); a nested run's tokens add to its parent's total, and the example's 4 times is scripted, not measured ([subagent example fact-check](../../../docs/plans/reviews/fact-check-openai-subagent-example.md)).

**Gaps.** No controlled benchmark compares planning patterns, and the 15 times figure is Anthropic's for one research system, not a law. The table of starting points is our reading, not a vendor claim.
