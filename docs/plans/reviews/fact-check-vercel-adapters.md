# Fact-check: the Vercel state adapters

This log checks the claims that `packages/harness/src/vercel/`, the harness README's Vercel section, `packages/harness/scripts/live-vercel.mjs` and todo N48a's outcome make. Claims were checked on 2026-11-25 on branch `todo/N48a-vercel-state-adapters`. Workflow SDK claims come from the pinned `workflow` 5.6.1 and its `@workflow/core` 5.6.1 and `@workflow/errors` in `node_modules`, Postgres claims from PGlite 0.5.16 run in this session, and `pg` claims from `pg` 8.23.1 and its `pg-types` 2.2.0. The build session could not reach vercel.com or workflow-sdk.dev, so claims only those sites could settle are marked ⚠️.

Legend: ✅ confirmed · ✏️ corrected while drafting · ⚠️ unverified or our own inference.
