## Goal

Write the chapter "Events and hooks: one event union, interceptors and observers" (`events-and-hooks`) for Part II, whose reader is a builder who has shipped an agent on some SDK and wants the design reasons. It answers one research question from the sources below, section by section, with checked listings, figures and analogies where they make a concept clearer, and ends with an exercise the reader can run offline. **Research question:** How should an SDK expose what happens inside a run, and where may outside code change it?

## Scope

**In scope**

- `apps/book/src/content/chapters/events-and-hooks.md`, with `switchTo: [B13, B16, B17]` and `opening: question` in its front matter
- Sections: One closed event union, with end events that carry the authoritative item; Six interceptors at model and tool moments; Observers that only watch
- Listings and at least one replay from: `verify/copilot-sdk/examples/08-hooks.ts`, `verify/langchain/examples/07-middleware.ts`, `verify/google-adk/examples/05-callbacks.ts`, `verify/pi/examples/04-steering.ts`
- Figures: a flat figure of the six interceptor moments on one timeline, with observers beside them; a flat analogy illustration of toll booths and traffic cameras
- Exercise: Move one hook from an interceptor to an observer and say what it can no longer do.
- `apps/book/fact-checks/events-and-hooks.md`, the chapter's claims log

**Opening:** question (the previous chapter in the outline opens with code).

**Analogies** (BK22: an approved pool, distinct across the book; use an analogy where it makes its concept clearer, drop it where plain explanation works better, and vet each one before drafting):

| Concept | Analogy | Where the analogy breaks | Illustration |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| event stream | a court stenographer's record | the stream also carries partial deltas that the final record supersedes | none |
| interceptors | toll booths that can stop, charge or turn back a car | an interceptor can rewrite the request itself, which a toll booth cannot | flat |
| observers | traffic cameras that only watch | an observer that throws can still break a run unless the SDK isolates it | none |
