The Aa icon and the font pairings

You chose H2 for the header and S1 for the settings. Two things are left to choose: the icon for the settings button and the typeface pairings in the list.

1. The settings icon

In the earlier mock the "A" and "a" were top-aligned, so the pair rode high in its button. Each option here is redrawn with both letters on one baseline and centred. The row on the right adds a dashed line through the middle of the button, to check the centring against the search, theme and menu icons.

I1 · A big A, a small a

The usual text-size mark. Both letters sit on one baseline, and the pair is centred in the button, so it no longer rides high. My pick.

I2 · Small A, large A

Reads as 'smaller / larger', like the AA in browsers' reader controls. Clear for size, less so for typeface.

I3 · One 'Aa'

The simplest: two letters at one size. Neutral, and the clearest sign that fonts live here, not just size.

I4 · A with arrows

An A with up and down arrows: a size-only control. Best if typeface were not in this menu, which it is.

2. Typeface pairings

Each pairing is a heading face and a body face, drawn at the manual's reading size. Book stays as the default. S1 can show about four to five pairings without crowding, so pick the ones to keep, and I would keep one serif, one sans and Readable. All are open-licence fonts on Google Fonts (SIL Open Font Licence, to be checked when we add them); we would self-host them and load a pairing only when it is chosen.
