More pairings: sans, and something different

Chosen so far: the I1 icon (a big A and a small a on one baseline), and the pairings Book (today's default) and Readable (Atkinson Hyperlegible). Below are six more sans pairings and three that are different in kind: a slab serif, code-style headings, and a rounded face. All are open-licence fonts on Google Fonts (SIL Open Font Licence or Apache-2.0 depending on the family, to be checked when we add them), and we would self-host them and load one only when it is chosen.

Your picks

S1's list shows about five pairings without crowding. Book and Readable are in, so pick up to three more, for example "Sans 2, Sans 6, Different 1". My suggestion: Sans 1 (Inter, the safest sans), Sans 6 (a sans with character) and Different 1 (Bitter, the most distinct of the rest). If you want a sans only, take Sans 1 and Sans 4.
