# Fact-check log: agent-building platforms report

The report's market map holds, and its recommendation stands, but one headline claim was wrong and two more were overstated. The AgentCore harness does take other providers' keys: it calls OpenAI's API, Gemini and any LiteLLM provider and keeps the keys in AgentCore Identity's token vault, so "no vendor lets you plug an arbitrary provider's key into its hosted harness" became "AWS is the one vendor whose hosted harness takes other providers' keys". Cloudflare's `byok_only` is an opt-in setting rather than a default, and Vercel's fallback to its own keys, which the draft reported only from a blog, is now confirmed from Vercel's docs as on by default. Twenty more corrections fix dates, quotations, counts and scope. Of 129 rows, 99 are confirmed (two of them only in part), 23 corrected and 7 unverified. The direct reads also resolved 37 of the draft's 40 "(search summary)" markers. The three that stay are all on Salesforce pages, which this session could not reach, and two new attributions mark a pair of launch dates and one test count that no primary page showed.

- **Date:** 2026-11-18
- **Document checked:** [agent-building-platforms.md](agent-building-platforms.md)
- **Method:** I fetched every cited URL with `curl --max-time 30` on 2026-11-18 and read the claim's wording on the page, using each docs site's Markdown form (`.md`, `/index.md` or `llms.txt`) where offered. Package claims come from `https://registry.npmjs.org/<pkg>`, `https://pypi.org/pypi/<pkg>/json` and the `@mastra/core` 1.85.0 and `v0-sdk` 0.16.7 tarballs. The CISA KEV claims come from the KEV JSON feed (`cisagov/kev-data`, catalogue 2026.11.18, 1,786 entries), and the OSV counts from `api.osv.dev/v1/query`, counting advisories whose GitHub severity is `CRITICAL` and whose `published` date falls in 2026. Microsoft's credit price comes from the Azure Retail Prices API. Paper numbers come from the arXiv abstracts and, where the abstract omits them, the arXiv HTML full text. `developer.salesforce.com`, `investor.salesforce.com`, `www.cisa.gov` (HTML catalogue), `openai.com` and `help.openai.com` refused the fetch or were outside the session's network, so their claims keep a marker. Nothing was installed or run, and fetched content was read as data.
- **Legend:** ✅ confirmed as written (a "(search summary)" marker dropped where the row says so) · ✏️ corrected in the report (the research notes keep their original text) · ⚠️ unverified or confirmed only in part
