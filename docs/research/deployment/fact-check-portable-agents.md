# Fact-check log: portable-agents report

The portable-agents report holds up, and its recommendation stands: seven seams make the harness portable, and E2B, Cloudflare Sandbox and Vercel Sandbox are the hosted backends to support first. Of the 122 rows below, 85 are confirmed, 31 needed a correction and 6 rest on inference or on pages that state less than the claim. The largest correction concerns our own plan. The harness plan already defines `SessionLock` and `KeyStore` as interfaces, each with only a file implementation, so two of the seven seams need new implementations rather than new interfaces. Four corrections change the sandbox comparison: Modal now has beta credential injection, Vercel's firewall rules can match on method, Cloudflare can also intercept HTTPS with its own CA, and E2B's deny list takes CIDRs only. Six fix quotations or attributions that did not match their source, including an Open SWE quote absent from the cited commit and a Cursor quote that was paraphrased. The rest fix dates, citations, prices and todo ids. Every Cloudflare Workers limit the owner flagged checks out as written.

- **Date:** 2026-11-18
- **Document checked:** [portable-agents.md](portable-agents.md)
- **Method:** for every number, version, date, status and behavioural claim, I opened the primary source. Cloudflare pages were read as their `/index.md` Markdown variants or as `llms-full.txt` (Containers, Sandbox), Vercel pages as their `.md` variants, Daytona and E2B through their `llms-full.txt` files, and other vendor pages as HTML reduced to text, all fetched with `curl` on 2026-11-18. Package versions, dates and licences come from `https://registry.npmjs.org/<pkg>` and from files inside the published packages on unpkg. Repository claims were read from raw files at the cited tag or commit. Claims about our plans, ADRs and earlier reports were read from this repository as it stands on 2026-11-18. Fetched pages were read as data. Nothing was installed or run.
- **Legend:** ✅ confirmed as written · ✏️ corrected in the report (the notes keep their original text) · ⚠️ inferred, or confirmed only in part
