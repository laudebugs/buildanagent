## What do our plans and ADRs commit to (SDK, harness, triage agent)?

### Takeaway
The phase ending 2026-11-18 produced three reviewed plans (SDK: 22 todos, ADRs 0001–0011; harness: 41 todos, ADRs 0012–0021; triage agent: 24 todos, ADRs 0022–0031). They commit to a TypeScript stack where we own a small streaming loop over our own `fetch`-based model gateway, keep one append-only tree-shaped session log as the only source of truth, sign every approval pause, fail closed on sandbox and budget, and gate releases on pass^k with cost beside every score. Building (milestones M5–M7) is the next phase and has not started.

## What are our planned differentiators?

### Takeaway
Six differentiators recur: (1) eight verified SDK references with offline harnesses and matching agent skills; (2) signed, durable, fail-closed approvals over a host-only HMAC key ring with quorum; (3) a reversible, auditable write layer, which is **researched and recommended but not yet in the plans**; (4) fail-closed sandbox defaults with credentials kept outside the sandbox via a loopback reverse proxy; (5) evals against a mini-swe-agent baseline with cost beside pass^k and pre-emptive USD budgets; (6) OTel `gen_ai.*` spans projected from the log with a pinned convention commit and content off.

## What do our deploy-anywhere findings say?

### Takeaway
A draft report (U11, in progress, not yet through the repo's fact-check and review steps) finds the SDK already portable but the harness bound to one host in six places, and proposes seven adapter seams, two runtime tiers, a hosted-sandbox ranking (E2B, then Cloudflare, then Vercel), and eleven cloud-coding-agent components our plans lack. All fifteen resulting plan changes are proposals for the owner.

### Cited Findings
- Status: the report lives in `reports/` with notes in `research_notes/Portable agents and cloud coding/` (cloud-coding-agents.md, runtime-portability.md, sandbox-and-credential-providers.md, webcontainers-and-browser-sandboxes.md); unlike earlier U-round reports, no `docs/research/` copy or `fact-check-*.md` exists at `d64577d` (our `ls`), so treat it as a draft.

## What does the repo say about building vs adopting a loop, and a thin-harness middle path?

### Takeaway

A "thin harness layer over an adopted library loop" is recorded only as a generic build-vs-buy option for other teams, not as an option considered for us.
