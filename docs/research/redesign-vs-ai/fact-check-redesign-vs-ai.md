# Fact-check log: redesign versus AI report

The redesign versus AI report holds up, and its thesis stands: no study we verified compares a model with a redesign of a workflow, the evidence on defaults, steps and search shows large effects from changes that need no model, and the owner can settle the question for one workflow with a pre-registered four-arm paired measurement. Of the 129 rows below, 87 are confirmed (✅), 20 needed a correction (✏️) and 22 rest on an absence, an abstract-only check or our own inference (⚠️). A row with two verdicts, such as "✅ for the figure; ⚠️ for the method", counts under its first.

Twenty corrections were made. Three came from the drafting pass: the Dell'Acqua et al. author count (row U24-24), the Madrian and Shea cohort wording (row U24-51) and MAP's two wordings of its 68% (row U24-32). Ten came from the Opus review of the first draft: the Brynjolfsson et al. agent split (row U24-8), the τ-bench 48.2% average (row U24-43), the Johnson and Goldstein page count (row U24-55), the jam study's base (row U24-69), the worked example's unequal reliability bars and median-scaled totals (rows U24-89 and U24-92), the "fetched every source" overstatement (row U24-108), the Chapter 1 comparison (row U24-120), the registry kind for Hammer (row U24-121) and the citation keys (row U24-122). The review also added three model-versus-search studies (rows U24-112 to U24-119). Five came from the Sonnet medium review: Pasquarelli et al. is a crossover and not randomized (row U24-123), Xu et al.'s server-log times (row U24-124), the Wilson upper bound of 0.85 (row U24-125), the extra queries and time under Spatharioti et al.'s highlighting (row U24-126) and how the search arms were built (row U24-127). Two came from the independent writing review: MAP's 83% and 38.7% are not shown to be the same group (row U24-128), and Baymard's abandonment list leaves out the 42% who were just browsing (row U24-129).

The ⚠️ rows fall into three groups. Two papers were paywalled, so Scheibehenne et al. (2010) and Parasuraman and Manzey (2010) are checked at their abstracts only (rows U24-70 and U24-74), and the report says so. Several rows are absence claims: no study compares a model with a redesign of a workflow (rows U24-3 and U24-118), Anthropic's post never mentions redesigning the application (row U24-39), no verifiable agent-vendor postmortem was found (row U24-40), and no independent check of Hammer's cases was found (row U24-68). The rest are our own design and inference, which the report marks as ours: the four questions, the metric definitions, the method and the reading of the automation literature.

The worked example's numbers are invented and the report says so. Its rows check only the arithmetic, which a script recomputed from the numbers printed in the report.
