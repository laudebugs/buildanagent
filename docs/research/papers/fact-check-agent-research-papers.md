# Fact-check log: AI agent research papers report

The agent-research-papers report holds up, and its two conclusions stand: the foundational agent-architecture lineage needs no new design, and three specific gaps, a skill's runtime control flow, a tool's actual code against its declared contract, and a coding agent's authority over CI/CD, are real and currently uncovered. Of the 151 numbered rows below, 118 are confirmed (✅), 10 are corrected (✏️, split between a design choice with no outside claim to check, a wrong or missing citation, and two overstated paraphrases caught while drafting U23), and 23 rest on inference, on absence, or on a page the fetch tool could only summarize rather than quote verbatim (⚠️). Each of the six research sections was drafted and fact-checked on its own, before the next was started, per this repo's rule that a long report is fact-checked section by section; the front-matter rows (136-149) were checked last, against the assembled report, and rows 150-151 were added during U23, a later pull request that re-read two papers' own text while drafting a build-guide citation. On 2026-11-22 issues #165 and #166 re-read SkillCFI and Truthgate in full and added one row each, `fix/165-skillcfi-tool-errors-1` and `fix/166-truthgate-stopping-1`, which narrow row 68's and row 145's readings of the abstracts (✏️): the 65% message also carried workflow facts, and Truthgate's 180 tasks are the impossible variants of 90 tasks. On 2026-11-22 issue #167 re-read the other 24 papers that the guides, chapters, todos and issues rely on, in full, and added 24 rows `fix/167-reread-papers-1` to `-24`: 18 narrow a claim (✏️) and 6 confirm it (✅); the narrowed claims are corrected where the documents stated them.

No correction changes the report's thesis. Two corrections fixed a date in our own triage notes: the Truthgate paper's submission date (row 145; our notes said 16 November 2026, the paper's own page says 17 November) and Wong's governed-ML-experimentation paper's submission date (row 96; our notes said 17 November, the paper's own page says 18 November). ## Link check

Every relative link and anchor in the report, this log, and the research notes resolves. A script extracts each Markdown link target that is not `http(s):` or `mailto:`, resolves it against the file's folder, and matches each `#anchor` against GitHub-style slugs of the target's headings. On 2026-11-19 it checked 51 links in the report (35 distinct targets, 5 of them with anchors), with no failures; it was run again against every file in [notes/](notes/) individually, also with no failures. External links were fetched during each section's own fact-check pass and returned their pages, with two exceptions already covered above: both of OpenAI's blog posts on SWE-bench Verified returned an HTTP error to every attempt, so the report makes those two claims only from a search-result paraphrase, marked `[secondary]`.
