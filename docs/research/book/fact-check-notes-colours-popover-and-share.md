# Fact-check log: the notes colours, popover and share report

The report [notes-colours-popover-and-share.md](notes-colours-popover-and-share.md) holds up. Its browser-support claims match MDN's compatibility data as of November 2026, its behaviour claims match the specifications or a Chromium 157 measurement, and its numbers match a recomputation. Ten rows are ⚠️: a Safari release the report does not rely on, a reading about anchor positioning, and eight choices that are the report's own: the share thresholds, the touch-button height, the requirement that a read-only visit writes nothing, the marker's placement above the line, the click target taken from paint order, the uniqueness test against the page's text, the marker container's positioning and the link's base address.

- **Date:** 2026-11-22
- **Document checked:** [notes-colours-popover-and-share.md](notes-colours-popover-and-share.md)
- **Method:**
  - **Browser support:** read from [MDN's browser-compat-data](https://github.com/mdn/browser-compat-data) `main` on 2026-11-22 (the data behind MDN's tables), file by file: `api/Highlight.json`, `api/HighlightRegistry.json`, `css/selectors/highlight.json`, `api/Document.json`, `api/Range.json`, `api/ResizeObserver.json`, `api/HTMLElement.json`, `api/Navigator.json`, `api/Clipboard.json` and `html/elements/a.json` (`text_fragments`). The MDN pages for text fragments, the URI fragment, the Popover API, `Navigator.share()` and `Clipboard.writeText()` were read in full for behaviour.
  - **Specifications:** [CSS Custom Highlight API Module Level 1](https://drafts.csswg.org/css-highlight-api-1/) (sections 3 to 7), [CSS Pseudo-Elements 4 §3.2](https://drafts.csswg.org/css-pseudo-4/), the [Text Fragments draft](https://wicg.github.io/scroll-to-text-fragment/) and WCAG 2.2's understanding pages for SC 1.4.1, 1.4.11 and 2.5.8, each fetched on 2026-11-22.
  - **Measurements:** contrast ratios use the WCAG relative-luminance formula, recomputed in Python; amber's ratios match those already in `docs/design/book-tokens.json`. Colour-vision simulation uses the Machado et al. matrices at severity 1.0 and CIE76 ΔE. Browser behaviour was measured in Playwright's Chromium 157.0.8103.78 on a local page; Firefox and Safari were not run, so every claim about them rests on the data above.
  - **Repository claims:** `apps/manual/public/scripts/notes.js`, `apps/manual/README.md`, `apps/manual/public/_headers` and the book plan's BK24, read on this branch.
- **Legend:** ✅ confirmed as written · ✏️ corrected in the report · ⚠️ our reading, our choice or a claim the sources leave open.
