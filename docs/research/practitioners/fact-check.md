## Writing review

The synthesis was drafted with `/anthropic-skills:writing` and reviewed with `/anthropic-skills:writing-review`. The review fixes changed wording, not claims; the three corrections (rows 8, 15 and 16) came from the re-checks above. The fixes: overlong thesis and conclusion sentences were split or trimmed, the repeated "That many…" openings in §1 were varied, two passives were made active, "sharpens" was replaced by "qualifies" to keep one term for one verdict, and the §4.3 foot now ends on what transfers rather than on a limitation.

*Follow-up, 2026-11-18:* the what-fails note now reads "one of the three threads from the corpus's final day" instead of "the last thread", matching correction 3. I also re-checked the §2 contradiction about the Claude Agent SDK's sandbox default in `sdk.d.ts`: `allowUnsandboxedCommands` is documented "Default: true", which lets the model request `dangerouslyDisableSandbox`.
