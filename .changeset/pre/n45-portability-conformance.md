Export the eight-seam portability conformance suite from `@buildanagent/harness/testing` (`PORTABILITY_SEAMS`, `portabilityConformanceCases()`, `runPortabilityConformance()`). This release also exports standalone suites for locks (`lockConformanceCases()`, `runLockConformance()`), egress (`EGRESS_DENIED_TARGETS`, `egressConformanceCases()`, `runEgressConformance()`), and protocol transports (`transportConformanceCases()`, `runTransportConformance()`).
